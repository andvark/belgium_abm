> **ILLUSTRATIVE STYLISED POLICY - NOT BELGIAN LAW; fiscal results are mechanical demonstrations, not estimates of Belgian policy effects | SCENARIO OVERRIDES: corporate_income_tax.rate=0.3**

> **STYLISED ECONOMY - behavioural and wealth parameters are assumptions, not estimates**. Population dynamics use *observed* Belgian life tables and fertility; labour, education and household flows are derived from 2025 cross-sections;
> wealth, behaviour, business and all tax/benefit values are assumptions. These results are **mechanical demonstrations, not forecasts and not estimates of Belgian policy effects**.

- Policy file `corporate_tax_plus_5pp` fingerprint `ab31422c302c1f99`, verified against law: **False**
- Max household cash-identity residual: 3.73e-09 EUR; invariant failures: 0
